## Description [4000]
Every day is a dot. Check off a habit and its dot fills in. Over weeks the dots become a wall, and the wall shows you what you actually did.

HabitDots is a calm, black and white habit tracker. No colors fighting for attention, no badges, no feed. Just your habits and your days.

HABITS
• Daily habits, specific weekdays, or N times a week
• Counted habits: 8 glasses of water, 3 sets, 20 pages
• Streaks that survive one missed day ("Don't miss today!" warns before a second)
• A full year of history on every card
• Notes on any day: what went well, what got in the way

WIDGETS
• Habit, checklist and inbox widgets for the Home Screen
• Check a habit right from the widget, without opening the app

INBOX
• Small tasks next to your habits: today, upcoming, overdue
• Due dates, reminders, and repeats (daily, workdays, picked days, monthly)

REMINDERS
• One reminder per habit at the time you pick, skipped on days it's done or off
• Mark it done or snooze an hour straight from the notification

YOUR DATA STAYS YOURS
• Works fully offline, no account needed
• Backups and CSV export in the Files app
• Restore a backup from the HabitDots Android app

HABITDOTS PRO
• Unlimited habits (free covers 4)
• Sync between iPhone and Android with Apple or Google sign-in
• Statistics
• Widget looks and compact habit cards
Pro is a yearly subscription with a free trial, or a one-time lifetime purchase. The subscription renews automatically unless cancelled at least 24 hours before the end of the period; manage it in Settings › Apple Account › Subscriptions.

## App Privacy (nutrition labels)
Nothing is collected unless the user signs in for sync (Pro). Then:

| Data type | Linked to user | Tracking | Purpose |
|---|---|---|---|
| Contact Info › Email Address | Yes | No | App Functionality |
| Identifiers › User ID | Yes | No | App Functionality |
| User Content › Other User Content (habit names, check-ins, notes) | Yes | No | App Functionality |

No analytics, no advertising, no crash reporting SDKs. Purchases go through Apple and aren't collected by the app. Tasks (Inbox) never leave the phone.

## Review notes
- Sync needs Pro. In the review build use the sandbox: buy "Yearly" (free trial) on the paywall, then Settings › Sync › Sign in with Apple.
- Account deletion: Settings › Sync › Delete account (Apple users confirm with Apple once more).
- No login is needed to use the app otherwise.
